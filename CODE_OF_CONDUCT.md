# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Disagree about technical decisions openly, but keep it
about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and
discriminatory language or behavior aren't welcome here.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that don't
follow this, and may block contributors for repeated or severe
violations.

Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer
directly (see the repo's About section for contact info).
